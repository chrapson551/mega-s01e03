0002 · Propose deduplication by evidence and failure mechanism

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-11-12 · 0 days ago

Context

Incident #4471 contains different date failures under similar subjects. Subject-only matching concealed the booking-v3 hint problem and treated anomalous one-day reports as confirmed parser failures.

Proposed decision

Replace decision 0001 upon approval. Read report bodies, resolve product and tenant settings, compare the path and expected/actual behavior, and merge only reports supported by a common failure mechanism. Subject normalization may suggest candidates, but cannot decide the merge. Deduplicate overlapping exports by report ID. Keep unknown causes separate and retain report-to-issue coverage.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and investigation. Engineers receive counts with explicit scope and uncertainty. Update the triage runbook when this decision is accepted; preserve the accepted original decision as history.
